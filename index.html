Common questions

The page connects but I can’t feel anything

Another app is probably holding on to the controller. Fully quit Steam (including the tray icon), DS4Windows, DSX or similar tools, unplug the controller, plug it back in and reload the page.

Does it work over Bluetooth?

Not yet. Bluetooth uses a different message format, so please use a USB cable for now.

Is this the same as the haptics in PS5 games?

Close, but not identical. The most detailed PS5 haptics are sent to the controller as an audio signal, which browsers can’t do reliably. This page drives the same motors the way PC games do, plus the full adaptive trigger effects.

Does it work on Linux?

Yes, but you may need a udev rule so your browser is allowed to access the controller’s HID device.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing about your controller is uploaded.
