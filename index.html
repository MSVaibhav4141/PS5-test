HD hapticsExperimental

The fine, detailed vibrations PS5 games use. Instead of motor commands, these are sound waves sent to the two actuators inside the grips, so you feel them rather than hear them. Needs a USB cable and Chrome or Edge.

Your browser will ask for microphone access. That’s only so it can show the controller’s name in its list of audio devices. Nothing is recorded.

Common questions

The page connects but I can’t feel anything

Another app is probably holding on to the controller. Fully quit Steam (including the tray icon), DS4Windows, DSX or similar tools, unplug the controller, plug it back in and reload the page.

Does it work over Bluetooth?

Not yet. Bluetooth uses a different message format, so please use a USB cable for now.

Is this the same as the haptics in PS5 games?

The rumble section works the way most PC games do. The HD haptics section is the real thing: over USB the controller also shows up as a 4-channel sound card, and channels 3 and 4 drive the actuators in the grips. That’s the same route PC games with full DualSense support use. It depends on your system exposing all 4 channels, which is why it’s marked experimental.

HD haptics says only 2 channels are available

Your computer is treating the controller as a stereo device, so the haptic channels get dropped. On Windows, open Control Panel, then Sound, select Wireless Controller, click Configure and choose Quadraphonic. On a Mac, open Audio MIDI Setup, select the controller, click Configure Speakers and choose Quadraphonic. Then restart your browser.

Does it work on Linux?

Yes, but you may need a udev rule so your browser is allowed to access the controller’s HID device.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing about your controller is uploaded.
